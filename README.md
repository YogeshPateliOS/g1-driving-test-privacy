# Privacy Policy: G1 Driving Test Ontario

> **This privacy policy has moved.** The current version is at **https://yogeshpatelios.github.io/g1/privacy/**

**Last updated: November 18, 2026**

G1 Driving Test Ontario ("the app") is a free app made by Yogesh Patel to help people prepare for the Ontario G1 knowledge test. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

## What the app stores on your device

Your language choice, bookmarked questions and quiz results are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me, and deleting the app deletes them. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number.

## Advertising

The app is free because it shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

- **Device identifiers**, such as Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and an app-specific identifier (IDFV)
- **Approximate location**, worked out from your IP address (not GPS)
- **Ad and usage data**, such as which ads were shown or tapped
- **Diagnostic data**, such as crashes and performance

Google uses this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure results. When you allow it, Google may also use it to show ads that are more relevant to you. Read how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Your choices

- **Tracking:** the app asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**. If you say no, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states):** where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy Settings** button on the home screen.
- You can also reset your advertising identifier in your iPhone settings.

## Children

The app is meant for people preparing for a driving test (16 and older). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Security

Information handled by the app is protected using standard measures, but no method of sending or storing data over the internet is 100% secure.
